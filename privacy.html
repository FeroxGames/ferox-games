Privacy

The short version: you can use the launcher and play every game without giving us anything. If you make an account, we keep a small amount of information, and you can delete all of it yourself, at any time.

Last updated 15 November 2026

If you don't make an account

The launcher doesn't collect anything about you. It has no ads, no trackers and no analytics. Your installed games, your "last played" times and your settings stay on your own PC.

The launcher gets its game list and game downloads from GitHub, and the download page is hosted there too. Like any website, GitHub can see your IP address when you download something. That is between you and GitHub, and is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

If you make an account

We keep:

  • Your email address, so you can sign in.
  • Your password, stored scrambled (hashed) by our sign-in provider. We can't read it.
  • Your display name and the date you joined.
  • Your playtime: which game you opened from the launcher, when, and for roughly how long, while you were signed in.

A sign-in token is also saved on your PC, encrypted with your Windows account, so you stay signed in.

What we use it for

  • Signing you in and showing you your own profile and hours played.
  • The community stats in the launcher: how many players are online, and total hours per game and per day. These are counts only. They never show names or emails.

Other players can't see your name, email or playtime. If we ever add something that shows your display name to other players, this page will say so first.

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't share it with anyone except the services below that run the account system.

Who else handles it

Accounts and playtime are stored with Supabase, a hosted database and sign-in service. Downloads and the game list come from GitHub, as described above.

Deleting your account

Open the launcher, click your name at the top right, then Delete account. Your profile and all your playtime are removed from our live database straight away. Your games stay installed on your PC.

If you can't get into your account, ask us using the contact link below and we'll delete it for you.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. If you're younger, please don't make one. You can still play without an account. If you think a child has made an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes too. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on our GitHub page. Don't put anything private in it, because issues are public.
